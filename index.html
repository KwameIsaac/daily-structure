Non-Negotiable Ground Rules

  • Work stops at 17:00. Staying late is the exception, not the norm. You're freelance — you set the boundary, not the client.
  • Lunch is mandatory. 13:00, away from the desk. Skipping meals isn't hustle, it's self-sabotage.
  • Meditate before the phone. 10 minutes. First thing. This is the anchor habit — everything flows from starting the day intentionally.
  • Gaming has a timer. 1.5 hours max on weekdays. Set an actual alarm. When it rings, you close the game. No "one more round."
  • Phone goes on the charger at 21:45. The scroll is stealing your sleep, your reading, and your mornings. Replace it with a book.
  • One errand per slot, two slots per week. Stop trying to "find time" for errands — they have a home now (Tue/Thu evenings).
  • The opportunity hour is sacred. 19:15–20:15. Business loan research, networking, applications, side project planning. This is where your future lives.
  • Imperfect days count. Hit 5/8 habits? That's a win. Missed the gym? Walk longer. The goal is consistency over months, not perfection today.
